Please note however that in this game many things can happen at the same time. I tried out-lining this guide in a way you can follow along, but you don't need to finish 1 step before starting the next.

# Your first step

Tap the tutorial NPC and you’ll be given your first Town Hall. Recommend placing it as far north as possible. Your town expands as it levels and you’ll gain useless water tile if you place it towards the south.


![][image2]



Tap these info dialogs to progress


![][image8]


Keep following the NPC’s directions as he unlocks more features for you. Often he will repeat same dialog a couple times before revealing more. Be sure to tap him multiple times.

Note: On a rare occasion, NPC will give no dialog. Which means your game is bugged. You'll need to clear your game data (or reinstall) and restart a new run.

I like placing Royal Room to the far east. That is the direction of the sea port where visitors will arrive from.


![][image3]



Many of the instructions are optional. The prompt to speed up construction with a hammer for example can be ignored.


![][image9]


While a few are mandatory such as building a city gate. You have to build it. Once done, I recommend destroying your fence. It's the easier way to allow your people out of town.


![][image10]


Then you will be given a Researcher. Research is how you produce more things to build. You will also unlock gears and consumable research as you meet their requirements (usually by having certain amount of items in your storage)

As weird as it sounds, your researchers do not need to be at their lab. They can be deployed outside town or even assigned to work in the mines. Their research will still progress normally.


![][image4]



The game will also give you decorations to research. Fountain, bench, flowers, etc. Try not to build too many, if at all. Townspeople in this game LOVE decorations. They'll prioritize using them over other works. Which can really hurt your town's productivity.


![][image5]



After research is unlocked, NPC will start to give you research for more storage when you run out of space. Grass storage full, you get grass research. Wood storage full, you get wood research. You get the idea


![][image6]



Running out of wood or grass for the first time will also trigger dialog to unlock production facilities for them.


![][image7]



Food, ore and mystic ore productions will come later.

# Reroll... or not?

Very soon NPC will unlock Intro (gacha) menu for you and offer 3 character tickets.

Recommend to only use tickets for this. Save diamonds for more important things.


![][image11]



It's really up to you if you want to reroll for an A or even S. S is only available twice a month for a few days each time.

Progression wise, A/S will not carry you. They start at level 1 and their lv cap isn't that much higher than your free D units. They can save you some diamonds in the future however if you want to marry them and produce children.


![][image12]
S rank Ninja available for 3 days


The NPC's prompt to draw units is optional. You can skip and save tickets for later.

# Sending out your units

After building a gate, you should have the Deploy feature unlocked. When you send out units, they will travel to the spot, harvest nearby lands and bring loots back to town. They will also pick up chests so go ahead and fetch all of them.

Deployment cost depends on distance and target zone lv. Higher zone has higher multiplier.


![][image13]



Remember! ***Do not*** deploy all units. You need idle people to rescue KO’d units.


![][image14]



Loots your units retrieve are considered Treasures which need to be analyzed to find out their contents. Merchant is your only unit capable of this for now. Recommend keeping him in town. He needs a Treasure Lab to perform this task. He will automatically pick treasure from storage and bring it to a lab.


![][image15]



Gears from chests can be given to your units from \
"Menu > Resident List"\
top left of your screen.


![][image16]


You will also learn about feeding items to your units. Focus resource on your guard. He will be your main fighter for a good while.


![][image17]


And eventually you will be tasked with dispelling fog! You do this by deploying next to a foggy area. Each foggy zone has a boss waiting for you.


![][image18]


When a boss is revealed, you can try to beat it which will get rid of the area’s fog for good. You can also Blocked Off to reset fog for more grinding.

Monsters that the boss spawns will attack your town during full moon nights. Blocking off is one way to prevent that.


![][image19]


When your units go low on HP, NPC should give you a healing facility. If you don't get it, you may need to progress your quest a little more.


![][image20]

# Energy

Energy do not regenerate on its own. Instead, we need to collect them from Town hall and Energy Mine which accumulate energy over time. We obtain our first energy mine from clearing 1 of the fog lv 2 (there are a few lv 2s)

Lv up mines will speed up energy accumulation.


![][image21]



There are 2 ways to collect energy. First is to tap on the icon manually.


![][image22]



And second is to tap on the + sign in the resource panel and choose "Collect" (not the Diamond one!). The game will automatically deduct energy from random storage without us having to chase them down.

# Items to keep

There are some items you may want to hold on to a few. Let's go through them together.

**Food:** snacks, sweets, burgers, bread, etc. No need to keep. Feed away.


![][image25]



**Seeds looking thing:** for growing wood and grass. I usually hold on to about 15-20 each.


![][image26]



**Upgrade materials.** boards, nails, ropes, brick. Also metals and cloths. You need them for leveling facilities. Only need a few at first and  the number increases as you go higher.


![][image27]



**Fruits:**

* Apple for spawning farm animals. hold on to 5-10 as you unlock this pretty soon.
* Orange for spawning monsters. This is quite late game, feed away for now.
* Others such as bananas, grapes - feed away.


![][image28]



**Herbs and meds:**  holding certain amount can unlock some research. Not crucial and these are quite easy to come by.


![][image29]



**Samples, tomes, scroll of knowledge:** unlock equipment research which can come in quite handy. I recommend hold on to 10 each for early game. They are generally easy to come by **EXCEPT** Armor Sample which is rare.


![][image30]



A lot of these items can be purchased from Material Shop but keep in mind the shop can run out of supply.

For full list of research you can unlock, please reference [Anis' interactive tool](https://kingdom-adventures-community-tools.vercel.app/research)

# Weapon shop

Important! Recruit female blacksmith. There are some early INT gears that only female can wear.


![][image31]



You get blacksmith as reward for clearing fog lv2 north west of your town. Recruit and build her a weapon shop. Weapons are crucial to your progress and you want to start earning coins asap.

Do note that blacksmith shop consumes ore


![][image32]
This is Ore. The red one to the right is Mystic Ore


I like lining up my shops along lower right edge of my town so visitors can browse every shop back-to-back. The sea port visitors will arrive from is to the east.


![][image33]



Important! you need iron ore to complete the shop. This will be given by the NPC but you need space in your item storage.

If your storage happened to be full, the ore will be lost. Luckily you can destroy the shop and rebuild and the NPC will give you another one.

We are too early to obtain this ore by ourselves.


![][image34]



Then place shelves in your shop.

How it works: when there's an empty shelf, shopkeeper will pick 1 item from the pool of everything she can craft and put it on that shelf. This pool expands with shop level and crafter's INT.

After an item has been crafted once, it's unlocked for you to buy from the Shop menu to the right of your screen. The amount you can buy is unlimit. As many as you have coins to pay for.


![][image35]



For now, keep 3 things in mind

1. Level up your weapon shop to 7. Try not to go higher.
2. Increase your blacksmith's INT
3. Once shelves are full, use the sea port to call visitors. They will clear your shelves, making you money, and open the shelves up for shopkeepers to discover more items.

Level 7 is when you unlock Novel. A crucial piece of weapon. I will cover this more in later section.

Gears are your source of INT which is the book icon. This particular one, F/ Alice Band is a female only headwear you can obtain very early that gives INT. The reason we recommend female blacksmith.


![][image36]



Same icon for food.


![][image37]



As you clear more fog, you'll obtain a Mage and an Artisan.

* Mage can open skill shop. Consumes mystic ore.
* Artisan can open furniture shop. Consumes wood.

Again I build them in straight line to give visitors easy access.


![][image38]

# Copper coins!

Beating fog lv 7 gives you access to the Port. You pay food to call visitors who will buy items from your shops. Leveling up Port will make ship stays longer and spawns more visitors.

First you need to pick the town that food will be paid from. Visitors summoned can still be directed to any towns you like.


![][image39]



Visitors need Info Board (we just call them *signs*) to lead them to shops. Take note of the dotted lines; you need to connect that line from port to your shop’s door (or town gate). You can chain signs together if they need more distance.


![][image40]



Visitors will pay visit to your Monarch and pay 1 copper coin each.


![][image41]



They will also visit shops and we're hoping for them to clear our shops clean: giving shopkeepers chance to discover new items on top of making money.


![][image42]



The first time you obtain copper coins, you’ll be given a Copper Box. Beware that if you destroy it, you do not get your coins back. Unlike resource storage that will drop their content on the ground.


![][image43]



Guess what. Your combat units can buy from your own shops too. Generating passive income!

One big difference is that they will not clear shelves. Also, by default, they do not buy from skill shop. To get them to buy skills, they need to equip accessory.

We will take advantage of these points to specialize a town for passive income in the future. More info in [Early Copper Town](https://jazas.gitbook.io/kingdom-adventurers/early-copper-town).


![][image44]



Fog lv 7 also gives you a Paladin. You have 2 combat units now! Highly recommend you pick oppsite gender of your guard.

Guard+Paladin are good compatible pair that you can breed for stronger second generation.


![][image45]

# Food and Mystic Ore

Now that we use food for calling visitor, you may find yourself running low all the time. Deploying cannot keep up. You may notice that you're in dire need for mystic ore as well.


![][image46]



**Mystic Ore (the red resource)**

Unfortunately there is no good way to get decent amount of them at this stage. You will need to rely on deployment to Sand and Stone biomes. If you see red deposit looking thing on the map, deploy people to dig them up.


![][image47]



**Food**

You're in luck because you can start producing food pretty early. You get a Rancher for clearing fog lv 13. Recruiting rancher unlocks Ranch (called Farm in research because translation lol)


![][image48]



Once you build a ranch, you will unlock Monster Feed research


![][image49]



Monster Feed spawns farm animals. Your ranchers fetch them back to ranch where they will be tended to before turning into food. If you need more hands on this, give your units Ranch Know-How skill.

NPC tells you Feed needs to be outside, however, you can actually build it in town too.

Feed spawns a limit amount of animals. When runs out, you need an apple to refresh. If you don't have apples, you can simply destroy and rebuild. You do get a surplus of food with rebuilding.

We will cover apple production in later section.


![][image50]



Level up Feed will make it last longer spawning more animals. However, we want to keep it's refresh time nice and short so don't level it too much. The feed will spawn Zoo animals in the future and it only spawn 1 per refresh.

# Master Smithy


![][image51]



You obtain Master Smithy from fog lv 11. You can now level up your equipment! You have given your smith INT gears, leveling up her shop and summoning visitors as suggested, right? Then hopefully you’ll have something to work with.

First. Get a pair of weapons to lv 5. You cannot feed the same weapon to itself, that’s why you need 2.

Wooden Stick is a really good choice. It gives decent exp, can be equipped by many jobs, plus is unlocked from the start (if I remember correctly).

The other one can be anything that costs 3 coppers. Fruit Knife and Tiny Knife are popular choice.


![][image52]



Feed stick to knife, feed knife to stick. You get the idea.


![][image53]



Your weapons will hit its first cap at lv 5. You will need either Kairo gears or diamonds to break cap.

First few caps are quite cheap diamond wise. IMO spending small amount to get Wooden Stick to lv 10 or 20 isn't such a bad deal. Don't go crazy with your spending tho as we will gain ways to obtain Kairo gears soon enough.


![][image54]



Next we will focus on unlocking Novel. It's a weapon that looks like a green book.

It requires weapon shop lv 7 and shopkeeper INT 49.


![][image55]



This is where picking female blacksmith as suggested will help. You should have obtained F/ Alice Band and D/ Scarf from clearing early fogs. The band can only be used by females. Get both to lv 5 and your smith will meet the INT requirement.

What if your smith is a male, you ask? Worry not. Try to clear fog lv 23. Use friend's help if you need to. You will obtain a C/ Magic Pendant which will cover all your early INT needs.

We will cover friends in later section.


![][image56]



You may be asking, why stop at lv 7 and why Novel?

1. Higher level shop will increase the pool of items your smith can craft. Larger pool means lower % for Novel to appear. It's not game-breaking but trust me RNG is already annoying enough at lv 7. No need to make it worse 😂
2. Novel gives INSANE amount of exp when fed to other weapons. Just take a look how much it gives at lv 1 compare to others. You will use it as feed for every gears you level up in the future.


![][image57]



So what's the strategy here?

I recommend taking your time. Wait for all shelves to fill up and call visitors. It's slow but you'll be earning this way.

If you're in a rush, you can also manually destroy and rebuild shelf. The resources needed  can be a problem this early in game.

# Friends

Clearing fog lv 10 gain you access to Friend Post Office. This enables you to add friends, make use of their units, and register your own for people to use.

You can seek friends from [Discord](https://discord.gg/DJGDjS6svb) and [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/groups/2548592225201545) group.


![][image58]



There's a couple ways to use friends unit.

Most simple is when you attack boss or caves. There is a panel for you to pick one from your friend list.


![][image59]



Energy for using a friend can vary between 35-150. This is based on your play time differences. The longer a friend has been playing compare to you, the higher his character cost. For this reason I recommend you try to research and build energy storage and aim for at least 600 energy total.

I cannot remember how to unlock unfortunately.


![][image60]



Another way is to use the furniture Friend Bed. I believe you are given the first one for free when you have a few friends added.


![][image61]



Place it inside any house will allow you to summon random friends from your list to stay in your town for a couple in-game days. Friends will defend your town from attackers and can be deployed like your own units.


![][image62]

# Caves

Now that we have access to friends help, we should discuss Caves a little bit because Monsters spawn from caves will attack near by town halls during full moon.

With friend's help, there's a chance for a cave to spawn in zones too high lv for your own people to handle.


![][image63]



There 2 main ways to deal with this.

1. is of course to use friend's help to clear high lv caves.
2. there are only limited amount of caves that can appear on your map. The number increases the more zones you've cleared. So when they spawn in low lv area, leave them alone. Hit the cave cap and they won't be able to spawn in high lv zone anymore.

# Upgrade Materials

Briefly mentioned in [Items to keep](https://jazas.gitbook.io/kingdom-adventurers/items-to-keep). These are materials needed for  leveling facilities.

For clarity. The game does use the term Ore in a couple places.

First are ones in resource panel. Hi-lighted left one is just Ore, the right red one is Mystic Ore


![][image64]



And then there are the Iron Ore, Bronze, Gold, that are stored as items. I'll refer to these as Metals to avoid confusion.


![][image65]



As you can see from the Town Hall example. We usually need both resource and materials to level our facilities. And a few will require coins too.


![][image66]



So how do we obtain them?

Early game, you have to rely on monster drops. Deploy units out to farm, or  if you're actively playing, defog and reset.

As you progress, you will obtain facilities to produce them. Clearing fog lv 16 gives you Tool Workshop. It's an interior furniture. You need to give lands to your units and place it inside their houses.


![][image67]



Only people living in the house can use its furniture. Tool workshop has a chance to generate nails, boards, bricks and ropes when used.

Some other furniture you will want to have are Tree Nursery which generates seeds for growing wood/grass, and Ore Workbench which generates all sorts of mineral ores.


![][image68]



Study Desk and Glittering Stone produce the same products as tool and ore workbench. These 2 can be leveled up which grant more exp per use..


![][image69]



Another not so easy to come by materials are cloths. These will drop quite rarely at first and you may have to rely on Material Shop for a while.

Drop rate increases significantly once you are able to reach Snow zones.


![][image70]

# Material Shop


![][image71]



Unlocks from clearing fog lv 10 (there are a couple lv 10 zones)

When you lack upgrade materials, you can grab them here if you need them in a pinch. Price goes up every time you buy. It will also run out of stock eventually with no way to restock. So I recommend using it only when you need important upgrades.

# Restoring lands

As the lands around your town turn into barren wastelands, you may be wondering how you can make them pretty again. Fear not. You’ll obtain Wasteland Guide that will rally your townspeople to restore them.

Lands that are restored can spawn additional resources such as trees in woodlands, ores in desert, mystic ore in rocky, etc. Can be quite helpful when you have no industry set up yet.

Note: the guide only works if you can see dotted line connecting to a city gate while placing it.


![][image73]



After the lands are fully restored, remove the guide. Other wise it will continue calling your people to the area and they’ll wander around doing nothing.

# Hatching Eggs

Note: Early-mid game, consider hatching for fun. They can't really contribute anything meaningful. I love having a duck in my town. Late game people only target hatch some very specific monster. Namely, feeding Cakes to hatch Kairo Kommander.

When you kill 30 of any particular monster, a shiny version of it will appear randomly on map. Kill it and it will drop an egg. Color can belong to the biome the monster is from, or randomly you'll get a rainbow one.

You can tell they're the shiny from the bling bling.


![][image75]



Eggs get picked up like ordinary loot. Bringing one back to town unlocks Egg storage and also stables. There are 2 kinds of stable.

* Monster Stables is the outdoor one. Pet assigned to it will help defend town when attacked. When KO'd, they'd run back to their stables by themselves needing no rescue which is nice.
* Monster Room is the indoor one. If the house owner is in boss attack squad or part of PvP team, he will bring all the pets in the house to the fight too.

Ranchers can build Monster House when given a land which can host more monsters than other houses. XL size can host 5 monsters.

Each color eggs have their own list of monsters they can hatch into. [The Wiki pet hatching table](https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Eggs_\(Kingdom_Adventurers\)) is a good place to check full list of this.

Put an egg on a stable, you get a timer until it hatches. You can simply wait for it to hatch just like that. Or feed it items to increase chance for certain monsters.


![][image76]



Let's say I checked this [wiki monster page](https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Monsters_\(Kingdom_Adventurers\)) and really liked Brute-Belly for some reason... How do we target it?


![][image77]



A great tool to help you plan this out is the [Egg Planner](https://kingdom-adventures-community-tools.vercel.app/eggs) tool from KA tool set, but, we'll do it manually here.


![][image78]



This tells us we have the best chance if the Balance stat is between 48-56. Flan is usually what we use for this stat. I'll list this below.

So we fed it flans until I get Balance into that range. Then we can tap Forecast to check my chance.


![][image79]



Brute-Belly is there on bottom left with "High" Chance. Something on the top right also has high chance. We can only hope for the best.


![][image80]



3 days later...... WRONG ONE DAMMIT.


![][image81]



After a pet is born and is put on a stable, you can "train" it by feeding it eggs. Feeding a pet eggs matching it's origin with lower star will increase its max level by 1. For this reason it is best to hatch one with the highest star you can find.


![][image82]



Popular feed for eggs. They are ones we can purchase from our own shop usually. Cake, Flan, Ice Cream are also very common drops.

**Attack**: Milk (+16)

**Defence**: Cake (+16)

# To Zone lv 34

Zone lv 22 and 34 are our next priorities to conquer.

* 22 unlocks weekly conquests. Our source of S rank gears and units
* 34 unlocks equipment exchange. Source of Kairo weapons for breaking gear lv caps.

Both features are HUGE for progression.

The 2 zones line up conveniently next to zone 16.


![][image83]



With lv5 gears, you should be able to clear fog. However, zone bosses are different story and you will most likely need help. Refer to [Friends](https://jazas.gitbook.io/kingdom-adventurers/friends) section if you need direction how to add friends.

On the plus side, these zones are not very high so you don't particularly need super OP friend who requires hundreds of energy.

**Weekly Conquest**

Another reason to reach out to communities. Each week, we are required to kill specific monsters. Every person in your friend list will contribute to this kill count.

Rewards are great. 500 diamonds, A or S gear, and an S unit.

Don't worry if you cannot kill the high level ones. As long as you get a couple kills in for the low lv ones, people are generally OK with that.


![][image84]



**Equipment Exchange**

This is where you can trade normal equipment for Kairo gears. Heads up - the interface sucks 😂 It can be quite hard to find the gears you want to trade with.


![][image85]



Anyway. This allows us to break equipment level caps without spending diamonds (we will be spending copper instead).

1 of your main goal from here is to level your Novel and Wooden Stick to lv 99. It can be quite tedious but keep at it. Once you get there, your rank D fighters will be able to tackle fog up to level 100 or so.

Good luck!

# Treasure Room

Clearing fog lv 21 unlocks the treasure room. You can purchase treasures which grant permanent boost to various things.


![][image86]



Don’t go crazy on buying them however as we have limited trophy/crowns. It will be very, very late game before you can craft them. Go for the important ones first: Book of Awakening and Town Building Textbook


![][image87]



**Book of Awakening**

Your character starts with an awakening cap of 5. Each Book increases that by 5. You need at least 2 books to raise your character cap to 15 to make breeding lv 999 offspring possible.

* First book when you unlock treasure room
* Another book becomes available when your highest town hall reaches lv 34
* Another book from clearing fog lv 88

**Town Building Textbook**

Increase level cap of your town hall. You start with cap of 20 while each book increases it by 5.

Another useful one is **Diamond Guide** which reduces diamond cost on everything.


![][image88]

# Planning your 2nd Town

We get a second Town Hall from clearing fog lv 20. Now that we can break cap our weapons and have friends' help at our disposal, we have a real mean to pave ways for expansion.

Before placing down your second town, make sure to obtain the Movers facility from clearing fog lv 23. It can move your units from town to town.


![][image89]



**A few things of note:**

1. You can destroy and rebuild town halls as many times as you like, free of charge.
2. You need to remove every building in the town before you can destroy Town hall.
3. Every Town hall you place starts at level 1.
4. Area can only belong to 1 town. Red town below loses almost ⅓ of what it could have claimed if we build it further away from green town.


![][image90]



There is no right or wrong way to place your second town but I do have a suggestion - use your 2nd town as an expansion towards Legendary Cave which is located in lv 120 zone. This zone may not be unlocked for you yet, but, trust me. It is there 😄 We all farm this cave to death 24/7. More on it in later section.

Center of the green area is a good spot imo. Try not to cover up  lv 61 and 82. Those are the 2 highest rocky biome. You won't be able to spawn some mobs for weekly conquest if you cover them up.


![][image91]



You will also notice how more and more upgrades are needing cloths. By expanding this way, you will be closer to Snow biome which is the best source of it. So plan to have facilities you want to upgrade in this town.

# Town Hall Farming

Or, TH Farming. It’s a simple trick for farming diamonds. Now that we have additional Town Halls, we can start exploiting this.

You get Town Halls from clearing fog 20, 41, 52 and 75

The game rewards us with diamonds every time we level one up. We take advantage of this by repeatedly level one up from 1 ⇒ 2.

Build. Level up. Destroy.

Build. Level up. Destroy.

Repeat.

We usually stick to 1 level because it’s fast. 1 ⇒ 2 only takes 3 seconds. You can go higher if you don't mind the wait. We gain 50 diamonds the first few times doing this. After that the number will reduce to 5 diamonds.

You may think, only 5??

Yes 5. However it’s very quick. You can complete the cycle in 30 seconds. That's 600 diamonds per hour. Tedious, but possible. Some players were able to automate this. Reach out on Discord for more info.

**Step-by-Step**

1. Recommend not to do it near full moon. Monsters can destroy part of your town and then you’ll have to spend time repairing before you can remove the town hall.
2. Pick a fighter, put him in the Mover facility.
3. Find exp source. Can be monsters around caves, shinies, Chaos Stone, etc. Chaos Stone is most consistent.
4. Relocate Mover facility closer to the spot.
5. Place a town hall very close to the exp source. Try not to trap monster under your town hall or your fighter won't be able to attack


![][image93]



6. Use a town with plenty of copper coins. Buy 1 Sturdy Board and pick this new town as receiver. Instruction on this below.
7. Add fighter to town. He’ll go after monsters within his movement range so jobs with long range are preferred.
8. Watch him kill 1. Wait for the EXP star thing to travel to town hall and the level up icon shows up.
9. Level up and Immediately move your fighter back to Mover.
10. Obtain your diamond.
11. Destroy town hall.
12. Repeat.

# Early Copper Town

You may have seen crazy copper town setups with massive XL shops generating tens of thousands of coins per hour. Yea we’re not there yet :)

I prefer L size shops because we can lay down carpets straight from crafting station to the shelves. Shopkeepers need to walk from station to shelf after every craft. Move speed boost from carpet is really helpful here.


![][image126]



The INT set is enough to help even D ranks craft every spells in game. What you need are more skills slots as outlined in [Essential jobs for next step](https://jazas.gitbook.io/kingdom-adventurers/essential-jobs-for-next-step)

On the plus side: idea is very simple.

* Place a shelf
* Check price
* Destroy and replace shelf if it's too cheap

On the down side: it's entirely RNG.

You decide what your minimum price is.


![][image127]



* 3 coin skills show up at shop lv 10
* 4 coin skills show up at shop lv 14
* 5 coin skills show up at shop lv 16
* most expensive skill, Chat III, shows up at lv 60

**Things to know:**

* DO NOT call visitors to your skill shops. You don't want your shelves you worked hard to put high price skills on to get cleared.
* Fighters need accessories equipped before they'll buy from skill shops.
* Domestic Production skill prevents fighters from buying.
* Use Restaurant Shelf. You unlock it when you give cook a land and open a... can you guess... restaurant. This shelf can hold 5 goods instead of 3.
* 3-5 shelves per shop is enough. No need to go crazy with 9++ shelves like some guide recommended. Once your shopkeepers start crafting higher price items, they can NEVER keep up with refilling that many shelves.
* You can tap a shelf to check it's goods price. Position to tap is a little weird; slightly beneath, as shown.


![][image128]



**Skills for shopkeepers**

For reference. You won't have access to all of them yet.

* Craftsmanship. Speed up crafting.
* Skills Craftsman. Extend crafting schedule. Shopkeepers normally craft in the morning. Skill Craftsman I extends that into afternoon. II extends all the way to evening.
* Domestic Production. Keeps shopkeepers in town.
* Move Speed Up + All-Out-Sprint.

# Complete Copper Town

The key to pushing our Copper town to the next level is Cash Register, obtainable from survey. Cash registers take care of customer payments, allowing your mages to craft uninterrupted.

Keep checking survey locations as you unlock more zones. You'll find them eventually. There are 8 (or 9?) available from survey so that's usually the number of skill shops we have in our final layout.


![][image154]



Build them on top of a shop's cashier.


![][image155]



IIMO the best bang of your buck shop level is **25**. You gained 6 spells that sell for 7 coins. After that, higher price spells are quite far and few between.

lv 50 and 60 gives you Chat II and Chat III. Totally optional skills but fun (or frustrating) to put on your shelf.

* 6 x 7 coins, lv 25
* 1 x 8 coins, lv 28
* 2 x 10 coins, lv 35
* 2 x 12 coins, lv 40
* 1 x 15 coins, lv 50
* 1 x 18 coins, lv 60

**Observe! Don’t go too crazy too soon.**

Higher price skills take longer to craft and, with your people actively buying, your shopkeepers might not be able to fill as many shelves as you hope. There is no reason to go XL shops with 9 shelves like some suggestions floating around the net.

My shopkeeper never made it to the last shelf.

Shopkeepers have a specific pattern on how they work. They work on 1 shelf at a time starting from left to right on row #1. After all on  #1 are filled, they move to the next row working in the same pattern.


![][image157]

* **C** is last to get filled. If your most expensive skill is on this shelf, there is a chance it may never get refilled. If this happens, consider removing some shelves ahead of it.

Having 30 shoppers is a good number to aim for. Monk, surprisingly, is also a shopper.

# Lv 999 2nd Gen

Now, you should have all the tools to make this possible.


![][image160]



You will need, roughly:

* 10,000 diamonds give or take.
* 200,000 copper coins.
* Enough awakening cap (15 at least)

The process itself is quite simple if you stick to good compatible parents. It just takes time.

* Pick A or S parents with A Compatibility
* A parents awakening combined = 29
* S parents awakening combined = 28
* Do not let their offspring born until they reach their awaking target. You can do this by simply not giving them crib.

That's about it really... If you want to try pairs that are lower than A compatibility, you can use my [Marriage Simulator](https://panupatc.github.io/Kingdom_Adventurers_Marriage_Sim/). Simply pick the jobs you're interested in and enter awakening values to see result.

**DO NOT** follow some advise floating around Reddit about letting first child born half way into your awakening process. At the end, you still would have spent the same amount of resource. Except you end up with 1 x 999 child instead of 2. A complete and utter waste of resource.

About awakening. It is how you increase unit's level cap. You do this from the training screen by feeding copies of units of the same job to your character. These copies must be in stock, not recruited to town.

We typically only use D ranks as feed. They can be purchased from Job Center. D ranks are much more cost efficient than using higher ranks.


![][image161]



Here's an example. From my [Simplified Compatible Parents sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1Ic1CIdFFoeU_wvmMuMPpd84DF3XCb0Ln_HPJ-qg1PrY)


![][image162]



Mage+Mage have A compatible. Produce Magic Knight. My Mages were rank A so they needed 29 combined awakening. Here I did 20+9.


![][image163]



And here's their child.


![][image164]



As simple as that.

The awakening can be any combination. 1+28, 10+19, 14+15 etc. 14+15 will most likely be the one you start with.

Parents levels don’t matter. The child will be born with random stat levels.

Job Center (unlocks from fog 35) is your main source of D ranks.


![][image165]



The center starts off with 20 units in stock. D ranks cost 100 diamonds each. After you buy all 20, you need to pay 10,000 copper coins to restock 3 more.


![][image166]



Buying A ranks is an option if you are looking for specific pairs to marry.

You can save some diamonds if you rely on S ranks from weekly conquest.  However, weekly conquest only rewards domestic jobs. I think we can all agree that awakened combat units are more useful than domestic ones.

**Royals**

Royals are offspring of your Monarch. They have higher base stats and higher growth than normal units. Also require 1 less awakening compared to other pairs.

* Rank A Monarch, 28 combined awakening
* Rank S Monarch, 27 combined awakening

Monarch can only be awakened with Scholars which you can obtain mainly from survey.

Be careful. Your monarch’s rank increases when your highest town hall reaches the following levels:

* Rank A: town hall 45
* Rank S: town hall 60

If you married your Monarch to an A and your Monarch turned S before you could breed your Royal, well, too bad I guess.

You can check Monarch's compatible pairs from my [Simplified Compatible Parents sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1Ic1CIdFFoeU_wvmMuMPpd84DF3XCb0Ln_HPJ-qg1PrY)

# Chaos Stone exp farming

I did not set this up until 3,200 zone. Lower level monsters didn't give enough xp to make it worth setting it up IMO.

Main target of this is for exp’ing 999 2nd gen units. If you go to [Data mine spreadsheet, in the Formula - FAQ tab](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1e5t0CMBgw2MOv1NRE-vNk3229p7dYg6yJAQ8YbhYnWk), you will see this illustration. This is a guide on how to place your stones and surrounding it with signs.


![][image168]



Yellow - Chaos Stones

White - 2 empty tiles. 1 is where you deploy

Blue - Sign boards

Sign boards should cover every tile within movement range of monsters from Chaos Stones. Their purpose is to prevent movements. Monsters can still spawn anywhere within range. After a bit of time, they will start moving and have no where else to go except those empty tiles.

If you see monsters roaming outside your signs, then you're miscounting the rows to build.

The cycle goes like this.

1. Unit gets KO’d and carried back to town to hopefully recover MP.
2. Spawned monsters move to those empty tiles while unit is healing.
3. your unit goes back and AoE to take down as many monsters as possible.
4. KO'd, repeat step 1.

Pic: monsters gathering on empty tiles


![][image169]



The most popular AoE skill here is the Myriad Arrow which, as you may have guessed, only works with bow. It’s unique in that it’s a multi-hit AoE that cost pretty low MP. Not great for PvP however because its radius is quite small.

IMO the easiest way to obtain the skill, is to breed some S/ Archers. They are born with the skill. You can use domestic S ranks from weeklies for this. Horrible affinity but doesn't really matter in this case. You don't need many. 2 or 4 will be enough. Strip their skills and turn them into shoppers.

Monk+Merchant, and Doctor+Researcher.

Alternatively, skill is also obtainable from the easiest floor of Wairo Dungeon and also a very rare drop from Legendary Cave.

**Essential skills for units exp'ing:**

* an AoE skill.
* Battle Maniac
* Facility Rec Up MP - recovering MP in this game is not guarantee. This skill increases the chance of it happening when unit is healing.

**Really good to have:**

* Perfect Dodge
* Stubborn
* EXP Up I, II, III - they stack
* Bow/Shield Resistance - for jobs that cannot equip them naturally

**For townspeople close to the setup:**

* Strategic Retreat - without it, lots of people will end up engaging while trying to pick loots.

Note. This setup only trains physical stats. HP, MP. Vigor, STR, DEF, etc. Caster jobs such as Wizard and Beast Tamer will need twice the amount of exp compare to melee jobs.

# INT training

In contrast. Caster jobs only require ½ exp for the 5 non-combat stats which INT happens to be part of.

We train INT using indoor furniture Magic Training Ground.

Build a Mansion. Mansion can fit many more beds allowing more casters to train. Also fill the house with as many Magic Training Ground as you can because they buff each others exp rate.

And of course you want to level up them up so they give more exp.


![][image170]



Magic Training Ground can be discovered from Furniture Shop which can then be bought for an unlimited amount.
